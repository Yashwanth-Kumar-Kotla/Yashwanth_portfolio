Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

AutoShorts is a personal automation tool owned and operated by Yashwanth Kumar Kotla. It creates YouTube Shorts and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data AutoShorts accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and how it is deleted.

YouTube API Services

AutoShorts uses YouTube API Services. By using AutoShorts, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses AutoShorts

AutoShorts is not a public service. The only user who signs in with Google is the owner, and it accesses only the YouTube channels that belong to the authorizing user.

What data is accessed

With the authorizing user's permission, AutoShorts accesses the following, for that user's own channels only:

  • Permission to upload videos to the user's YouTube channel.
  • Channel and video metadata, read only, such as channel ID, video titles, descriptions, and upload status.
  • YouTube Analytics reports, read only, such as views, watch time, and audience retention for the user's videos.

AutoShorts also stores the OAuth access and refresh tokens Google issues, so it can act on the user's behalf without asking them to sign in every time.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish videos to the owner's own channels and to measure how those videos perform. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

Data accessed through AutoShorts is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising.

Storage, retention, and deletion

  • OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored locally on the owner's computer or on infrastructure the owner controls.
  • Stored data is kept only as long as it is needed to publish videos and measure their performance.
  • Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as YouTube's policies require.
  • When access is revoked, or when deletion is requested by email, all stored tokens and YouTube data for that account are deleted within 7 days.

Cookies and tracking

AutoShorts does not use cookies or tracking technologies to collect user data. These web pages do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How to revoke access

You can revoke AutoShorts' access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page. After access is revoked, AutoShorts can no longer upload videos or read data, and stored data is deleted as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will take effect only after they are posted here.
